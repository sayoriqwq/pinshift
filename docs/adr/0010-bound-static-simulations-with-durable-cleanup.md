# Bound Static Simulations with independent durable cleanup

Every Applied Simulation receives an explicit Simulation Lease. The Pinshift app offers exactly 15, 30, and 60 minutes, defaults to 15 minutes, and has no indefinite option. An acknowledged extension adds 15 minutes to the current authoritative expiry without changing the Simulation Generation or reapplying coordinates, capped at one hour from the extension acknowledgement. Lease duration remains independent of Controller Link server duration.

A successful lifecycle-aware Apply is allowed only while the independent Cleanup Guardian is healthy and after the Mac durably records the matching Cleanup Obligation. The Guardian makes cleanup due at the earliest of the authoritative lease deadline, orderly foreground server shutdown, or 30 continuous seconds without the matching server-owner heartbeat. iOS App backgrounding and client-side Controller Link interruptions do not expire that heartbeat. The Guardian is restarted after login and reloads the durable journal; if the Mac or matching Active Test Device is unreachable, it retains Cleanup Pending and retries after recovery.

Stop Intent and pending lease extension are retained by the Pinshift app and redelivered with stable generation and request identities. Immediate restore, lease expiry, heartbeat loss, controller startup/shutdown, Guardian recovery, and `rl-reset` converge on the same idempotent clear workflow. Stopped Simulation still requires the successful public `devicectl` clear acknowledgement defined by ADR-0008. An iOS app cannot perform that developer-service clear itself, so a powered-off or unreachable Mac and device remain a physical recovery boundary.
